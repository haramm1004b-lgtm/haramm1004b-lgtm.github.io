About

goodinfo4you is a Japan-based content brand run by a creator in his 50s with a full-time job. We publish hands-on tests of AI tools and workflow automation for solo founders, freelancers, and small teams — mainly readers aged 35–60 who want tools that save real time, not hype.

Every review is built around a simple question: how many minutes did this actually save, on a real task?

What we publish

  • Hands-on tests — we run the tool on a real task and report the measured result.
  • Short videos — YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels showing the workflow.
  • Introductions — products we have not used yet are clearly presented as introductions, never as personal experience.
  • Workflow patterns — repeatable automation recipes for small teams.
